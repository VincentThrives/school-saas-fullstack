Scans per day

Set 1 for entry-only tracking (parents get a single "entered school" notification per child per day). Set 2 to also track exit — the 2nd scan of the day is treated as departure, but only if it lands at or after the "Exit window starts at" time below. Any extra taps beyond this quota are silently ignored.

Meaningful scans per day 1 — Entry only 2 — Entry + Exit

Time windows

Scans before "Exit window starts" are treated as entry (arrival); scans at or after are treated as exit. A scan past the "Late arrival cutoff" is stored as PRESENT with a small "Late · HH:MM" badge on the roster — never as an ABSENT.

Late arrival cutoff HH:mm, 24-hour Exit window starts at HH:mm, 24-hour

Auto-mark absent

When enabled, a scheduled job runs at the configured time each day and marks any student who hasn't scanned in yet as ABSENT. Parents get an in-app notification ("Your child did not come to school today"). SMS is not fired yet — that plugs in once the DLT template is registered on the super-admin SMS panel.
